Additional QA Procedures - Key Responsibilities

QUAL3091, QUAL3130, QUAL4000

📐 Review of Technical Drawings (QUAL3091)

  • PM/POR: Accountable & Responsible for reviewing drawing sets/series
  • PM/POR: Responsible for completing drawing review form
  • PM/POR: Responsible for tracking revisions

📁 Control of Project Records (QUAL3130)

  • PM: Accountable for ensuring record compliance
  • PM: Accountable for assigning record storage responsibilities
  • IT: Responsible for maintaining servers/backups
  • Originator/Reviewer: Responsible for applying naming conventions
  • PM: Responsible for duplicating client-hosted DMS records internally

✅ Corporate QA Program Roles (QUAL4000)

  • PM: Accountable & Responsible for implementing CQAP
  • CQAO: Responsible for conducting audits/surveillance
  • CQAO: Accountable & Responsible for assigning QA training
  • PM/CQAO: Accountable for halting services for QA issues

🔍 Key Insight: The PM carries the highest accountability burden across all QA processes, being accountable for document decisions, comment resolution, record compliance, and CQAP implementation. The POR holds technical accountability, while the PP serves as the escalation point when conflicts involve the POR. This creates clear escalation paths but also concentrates decision-making responsibility heavily on the PM role.
⚠️ Process Observation: Multiple people are "Responsible" for the same tasks (e.g., OR and RV both responsible for resolution facilitation), which can create confusion about who actually does the work. Digital tools could clarify this by automatically routing tasks to the correct person based on workflow stage.
