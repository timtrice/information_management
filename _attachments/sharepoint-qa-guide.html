📋 Document Review in SharePoint

👤 FOR REVIEWERS - Your Daily Workflow
1 Check Email/Teams
You'll get an automatic notification with a direct link to the document
2 Click Review Link
Opens directly to the document - no searching required
3 Review & Comment
Add comments directly on document. Check the QA boxes that appear
4 Click Approve/Reject
That's it! System records everything automatically
💡 Time Saver: No more printing, no more QA forms, no more filing. Review from anywhere - office, home, or field!
✏️ FOR ORIGINATORS - Submitting Documents
1 Upload to SharePoint
Drag & drop your document to the project folder
2 Fill Quick Info
Select document type and reviewer from dropdown menus
3 Auto-Notification
System automatically emails reviewer - you're done!
4 Track Progress
See real-time status in the "My Documents" view
