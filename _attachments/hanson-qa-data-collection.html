🏗️ QUAL3093 & QUAL3094 - Construction Submittal Forms

Shop drawings, delegated design, and submittal tracking
📋 QUAL3093.01 - Log of Submittals
Data Tracked: Submittal number, description, receipt date, category (actionable/informational), review status, return date
Who Populates: POR or delegate
📋 QUAL3093.02 - Traditional Review of Submittals
Review Actions: No Exceptions, Furnish as Corrected, Revise & Resubmit, Submit Specified Item, Rejected, For Information Only
Who Signs: POR Reviewers (if directed)
📋 QUAL3093.03 - Nontraditional Review of Submittals
Used When: Contract documents prepared by others (not Hanson)
Documentation: Actions documented on form only (no stamp)
📋 QUAL3094.02 & .03 - Delegated Design Reviews
Review Actions: No Comments, See Comments, Resubmit (Non-compliance), Resubmit (Incomplete)
Critical Check: Must verify CDOR professional authentication
