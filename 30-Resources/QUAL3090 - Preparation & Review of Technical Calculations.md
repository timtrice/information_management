### 1.0 Purpose

- Provides requirements for preparation, review and approval of Hanson's technical calculations
- Part of Corporate Quality Assurance Program (CQAP)
- Applies to all Hanson projects unless superseded by project-specific requirements

### 2.0 Scope

**Applicable to:**

- Technical calculations for analysis, design, quantities, and opinions of probable construction costs

**Not applicable to:**

- Calculations for proposals
- Administrative purposes calculations
- Field calculations (unless directed by PM or POR)

### 3.0 Key Definitions

- **Originator**: Person who performs and documents calculations
- **Professional of Record (POR)**: Licensed professional with responsible charge
- **Reviewer**: Person other than originator who reviews calculations
- **Technical calculations package**: Form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]] + calculations + supporting documentation
- **Professional authentication**: Process of applying seal, certification, signature, date

### 4.0 Responsibilities

**Professional of Record/Task Manager/Sr. Team Member:**

- Designate personnel to originate and review calculations
- Designate scope of review required
- Facilitate resolution between originator and reviewer
- Perform management review and complete form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]
- Provide professional authentication as required

**Originator:**

- Prepare draft technical calculations
- Initiate form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]
- Perform and document calculations
- Address reviewer comments
- Revise calculations under POR direction

**Reviewer:**

- Review calculations package per designated scope
- Document review comments
- Reach agreement with originator on comment disposition

**Project Principal:**

- Facilitate resolution if POR is originator or reviewer

### 5.0 Procedure Steps

**Step 1: Performing Calculations**

- Originator creates stand-alone technical calculations package including:
    - Form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]
    - Statement of purpose
    - Statement of assumptions
    - References for formulas, methods, computer programs
    - Results of calculations
    - References for graphs, charts, design curves
    - Copy of spreadsheets with spot-check hand calculations
    - Input data and computer output

**Step 2: Assigning Reviewer**

- Originator completes general information on form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]
- Identifies type of calculations and scope of review
- Submits package to reviewer

**Step 3: Performing Reviews**

- Review can be hard copy with manual comments or electronic with digital comments
- Reviewer performs review per assigned scope:
    - **Math check**: Detailed check of each calculation
    - **Spreadsheets**: Independent hand checks of representative sample
    - **Software applications**: Check input data accuracy and output reasonableness
- Reviewer marks revisions directly on package
- Reviewer completes form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]

**Step 4: Resolving Comments**

- Originator reviews proposed revisions and comments
- Documents dispositions and makes necessary revisions
- If disagreement, POR resolves (or project principal if POR is involved)
- New calculations from reviewer comments must undergo same review process

**Step 5: POR Approval**

- POR completes management review
- Signs and dates form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents]]
- Provides professional authentication if required
