## 1.0 Purpose

- Describes the process for traditional review of construction contract delegated-design submittals received from contractors or owner's representatives
- Part of the Corporate Quality Assurance Program approved by CQAO and President
- To be implemented for all projects unless superseded by client/contract/project-specific requirements

## 2.0 Scope

- **Traditional Review**: When contract documents specifying delegated design have been prepared by Hanson as the Professional of Record (POR)
- **Nontraditional Review**: When contract documents have been prepared by a POR other than Hanson (see Attachment A for modifications)
- **Exclusions**: Does not apply to other construction contract submittals (covered by [[QUAL3093 - Review of Construction Contract Submittals Traditional and Nontraditional]])

## 3.0 Definitions

Key terms include:

- **Contract documents**: Agreement, addenda, specifications, drawings, etc.
- **Contractor**: Person/entity awarded construction contract
- **Contractor's Designer of Record (CDOR)**: Entity retained by contractor to design project elements
- **Delegated-design submittal**: Submittal providing design/certification by design professional other than Hanson
- **Design intent**: Ideas, concepts, and criteria defined by client through contract documents
- **Professional Authentication**: Process of authenticating documents as instruments of service
- **Professional of Record (POR)**: Licensed professional with responsible charge for project deliverables

## 5.0 Responsibilities

### 5.1 Hanson Professional of Record

- Tracking and categorizing submittals
- Maintaining form [[QUAL3093.01]]/[[QUAL3093.01|QUAL3094.01]]
- Confirming contractor review and approval
- Confirming professional licensure and authentication by CDOR
- Designating reviewer and establishing schedule
- Communicating scope of review
- Providing completed forms or stamped submittals to contractor

### 5.2 Reviewer

- Reviewing contractor submittals per assigned scope
- Utilizing review checklists (optional)
- Documenting review comments
- Completing all form fields

## 6.0 Procedure

### 6.1 Receipt and Tracking of Submittals

- Governed by [[QUAL3093 - Review of Construction Contract Submittals Traditional and Nontraditional]]
- Only accept from general contractor or CDOR with prior contractor notification
- All delegated design submittals are "actionable"
- Maintain transmittal documentation in digital project records

### 6.2 Qualification of Submittal for Review

POR confirms:

- Submittal associated with delegated-design requirements prepared by Hanson
- Contractor review and approval stamp/indication
- Professional licensure confirmation and CDOR authentication
- Certificate stating compliance with performance/design criteria
- Listing of codes, loads, design assumptions used

### 6.3 Scope of Review and Schedule

- Focus on confirming delegated design meets performance requirements
- Note suspected errors as "for information only"
- Final design decisions are CDOR's responsibility
- Schedule dependent on contractor timeliness but consistent with project schedule

### 6.4 Review of Submittal

- POR/PM communicates scope and schedule to reviewer
- May use optional checklists
- Document using form [[QUAL3094.02]] or digital stamp
- Complete all form fields (1-14)

### 6.5 Review by POR

- POR reviews for completeness
- Evaluates compatibility with Hanson's limited scope

### 6.6 Review Stamp

- May use review stamp (Figure 1) in lieu of form [[QUAL3094.02]]
- Applied manually or digitally (preferably red)
- Not placed on blank pages or reverse sides
- Lock digital submittals before returning

### 6.7 Return of Submittal to Contractor

- Provide record of transmittal with completed form or stamped submittal
- Examples: letter, email, electronic portal confirmation

## 7.0 Records

Records maintained per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]]:

- Form [[QUAL3093.01|QUAL3094.01]] (or alternate)
- Copy of form [[QUAL3094.02]] (if applicable)
- Copy of stamped submittal (if applicable)
- Copies of transmittal letters and correspondence

## Review Stamp Actions

**Figure 1 - Review Stamp Action Definitions:**

1. **NO COMMENTS**: Design appears compliant and complete
2. **SEE COMMENTS**: For information only
3. **RESUBMIT (NON-COMPLIANCE)**: Design not compliant with contract requirements
4. **RESUBMIT (INCOMPLETE)**: Design doesn't completely address requirements

## Attachment A: Nontraditional Review Modifications

- For when contract documents prepared by POR other than Hanson
- Hanson POR must be assigned for review
- Limited scope: check conformance with contract documents and design intent
- Use form [[QUAL3094.03]] exclusively
- No stamp available for nontraditional review
- Return to client or contractor with transmittal record
