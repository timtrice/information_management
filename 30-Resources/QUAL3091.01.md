## Summary

The Manager Review of Drawings form (QUAL3091.01) standardizes how project managers or Professionals of Record document reviews of technical drawing sets. It records sheet numbers, revision designations, dates, and titles for each drawing. It applies to interim milestones and final deliverables, supporting both digital and handwritten signatures.

## Details

### Form Structure

#### Header Information

- Captures project number, project name, drawing set type or series, and review status.
- Records the reviewer (PM or POR) and date of the review.

#### Drawing Listing

1. Provides a table for drawing/sheet number, revision designation, date, and title.
2. Lists each drawing in the set or series under review.

### Usage and Instructions

#### General Notes

- Explains the form’s purpose and acceptable methods for signatures.
- Applies to both interim and final submissions and supports digital or handwritten entries.

#### Field Instructions

1. Defines how to complete key fields such as project number, name, and drawing set.
2. Guides entry of revision designations and drawing dates.
3. Details reviewer signature requirements to confirm completion.
