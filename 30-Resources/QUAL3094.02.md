## Summary

Form QUAL3094.02 provides a standardized method to document Hanson’s traditional review of delegated-design submittals when a graphical review stamp cannot be applied. It captures submittal identification, review findings, and authorizing signatures in accordance with the delegated-design submittal review procedure.

## Details

### Purpose

#### Overview

- Describe the process for documenting Hanson’s traditional review of delegated-design submittals when marking the submittal itself is impractical  
- Serve as an instrument of record when the digital or paper review stamp cannot be used  

### Scope

#### Applicability

- Applies to submittals prepared under Hanson’s contract documents and received from the contractor or Contractor’s Designer of Record for components designed under delegated-design provisions  
- Does not apply to other types of contract submittals, which are governed by procedure QUAL3093  

### Responsibilities

#### Hanson Professional of Record (POR)

1. Track and categorize delegated-design submittals using form [[QUAL3094.01]]  
2. Verify prior contractor review and authentication by the CDOR  
3. Confirm submittals pertain to portions of the work designed by Hanson  
4. Appoint qualified reviewer(s) and establish a review schedule  
5. Provide the completed form QUAL3094.02 and a record of transmittal to the contractor  

#### Reviewer

1. Conduct the technical review per the scope defined by the POR  
2. Use checklists if provided  
3. Document review actions on form QUAL3094.02 or via the standard review stamp  
4. Complete all required fields and sign the form  

### Procedure

#### Receipt, Tracking and Qualification

1. All delegated-design submittals are logged and date-stamped upon receipt; actionable submittals are identified  
2. POR confirms each submittal is associated with Hanson’s contract documents, bears contractor approval, and, where required, includes CDOR authentication and design criteria  

#### Review and Documentation

1. Review actions are defined as:  
   - NO COMMENTS: Submittal appears compliant  
   - SEE COMMENTS: For information only  
   - RESUBMIT (NON-COMPLIANCE): Does not meet contract requirements  
   - RESUBMIT (INCOMPLETE): Missing required information  
2. If the review stamp cannot be applied, form QUAL3094.02 is completed to capture the same action definitions and authorizations  
3. The completed form, or stamped submittal, is returned with a transmittal record (letter, email printout, portal confirmation, etc.)  

### Records

#### Retention

1. Retain form [[QUAL3094.01]] (submittal log)  
2. Retain copy of form QUAL3094.02 (if used)  
3. Retain copy of stamped submittal (if used)  
4. Retain transmittal records and correspondence
