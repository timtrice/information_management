## Summary

Form QUAL3090.01 serves as the standard review and documentation record for both technical calculations and technical documents. It captures project metadata, reviewer assignments, review scope, comments, dispositions, and management approvals in alignment with procedures [[QUAL3090 - Preparation & Review of Technical Calculations]] and [[QUAL3092 - Preparation and Review of Technical Documents]].

## Details

### Form Structure

#### Identification Fields

- Field 1: Type of review (Calculation ([[QUAL3090 - Preparation & Review of Technical Calculations]]) or Document ([[QUAL3092 - Preparation and Review of Technical Documents]]))
- Field 2: Project number
- Field 3: Title of the calculation or document
- Field 4: Document ID number (optional)
- Field 5: Professional of Record (POR)
- Field 6: Task Manager (TM) or Senior Staff under POR
- Field 7: Originator name
- Field 8: Date review initiated

#### Review Assignment Fields

- Field 9: Type of Calculation (Hand Calculation; Spreadsheet; Software Application; Other)
- Field 10: Scope of Calculation Review (Math Check; Method of Analysis/Formula; Assumptions; Input Information; Comparison with Previous Calculations; Hand Calculations for Spreadsheet; Other)
- Field 11: Type of Document (Report; Specification; Software/Code; Technical Memo; Technical Procedure; Planning Document; Lab/Test Report; Other)
- Field 12: Scope of Document Review (Narrative; Method of Analysis/Approach; Assumptions; Input Information; Comparison with Previous Documents; Include Document Attachments; Other)

#### Reviewer and Comments Fields

1. Field 13: Name(s) of Reviewer(s)
2. Field 14: Reviewer comment indicator (Yes or No), signature, and date
3. Field 15: Originator comments to Reviewer(s) (optional)
4. Field 16: General review comments (optional)
5. Field 17: Disposition by Originator, signature, and date
6. Field 18: Acceptance of Dispositions by Reviewer(s), signature, and date

### Review Completion and Approval

#### Final Review Fields

- Field 19: Editorial Review (Technical Documents Only), date
- Field 20: Management approval by POR or TM or Senior Staff, signature, and date
