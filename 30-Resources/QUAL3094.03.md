## Summary

Quality Procedure QUAL3094.03 establishes the nontraditional review process for delegated-design submittals prepared by external professionals. It confines Hanson’s review to checking conformance with contract documents and design intent, documents results on form QUAL3094.03, and mandates record‑keeping of submittals and transmittals. Responsibilities, scope, and distribution steps are defined.

## Details

### Procedure Modifications for Nontraditional Review

#### Tracking and Qualification of Submittals

- External delegated-design submittals are accepted only from the contractor or contractor’s designer of record.
- Project Officer Representative confirms contractor review and professional authentication before Hanson’s review.
- Review scope is limited to checking conformance with contract documents and design intent.

#### Distribution Steps

1. Complete form QUAL3094.03 and attach the stamped record set.
2. Send the review package with a signed transmittal record to the contractor or client.
3. Archive transmittal records and correspondence in digital project files.

### Records

#### Documents to Maintain

- Submittal log (form [[QUAL3094.01]] or alternate).
- Completed form QUAL3094.03 documenting nontraditional review.
- Transmittal records and related correspondence.

#### Record Retention

1. Maintain records in accordance with [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]].
2. Adhere to corporate retention periods per [[CORP1300 - Records Management Manual]].
