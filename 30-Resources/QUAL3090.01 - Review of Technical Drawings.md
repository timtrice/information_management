### Header Information (Fields 1-8)

1. **Type**: Check if Calculation ([[QUAL3090 - Preparation & Review of Technical Calculations]]) or Document ([[QUAL3092 - Preparation and Review of Technical Documents]])
2. **Project No.**: Hanson project number including subprojects
3. **Title**: Title of calculation/document being reviewed
4. **Document ID No.**: Unique identifier (optional)
5. **Professional of Record**: Licensed professional in responsible charge
6. **TM or Sr. Staff**: Task manager or senior staff under POR direction
7. **Originator**: Name of Hanson employee serving as originator
8. **Date**: Date review process is initiated

### Reviewer Assignment and Review (Fields 9-18)

**For Calculations (Fields 9-10):** 9. **Type of Calculation**:

- Hand Calculation
- Spreadsheet
- Software Application
- Other

10. **Scope of Calculation Review**:
    - Math Check
    - Method of Analysis/Formula
    - Assumptions
    - Input Information
    - Comparison with Previous Calculations
    - Hand Calculations for Spreadsheet
    - Other

**For Documents (Fields 11-12):** 11. **Type of Document**: - Report - Specification - Software/Code - Tech. Memo - Tech. Procedure - Planning Doc - Lab/Test Report - Other

12. **Scope of Document Review**:
    - Narrative
    - Method of Analysis/Approach
    - Assumptions
    - Input Information
    - Comparison with Previous Documents
    - Include Document Attachments
    - Other

**Review Documentation (Fields 13-18):** 13. **Reviewer(s)**: Names of assigned reviewers 14. **Comments**: Yes/No indicator, signature, and date for each reviewer 15. **Originator Comments to Reviewer**: Optional field for clarifications 16. **General Review Comments**: Optional field for logging reviewer comments 17. **Disposition by Originator**: Signature and date confirming comment disposition 18. **Acceptance of Dispositions by Reviewer(s)**: Signatures and dates confirming satisfactory resolution

### Review Complete (Fields 19-20)

19. **Editorial Review**: For technical documents only - signature and date
20. **POR or TM or Senior Staff**: Final signature and date for management review

### Critical Requirements

- Form must be completed for all technical calculations under scope
- All signatures required (can be handwritten or digital per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]])
- Review must be by personnel independent of originator
- All comments must be documented and resolved
- POR approval required before release
- Professional authentication required for instruments of service
- Records maintained per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]]

This comprehensive system ensures quality control through independent review, proper documentation, and management oversight of all technical calculations.
