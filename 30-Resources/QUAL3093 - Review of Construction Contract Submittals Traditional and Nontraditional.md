## 1.0 Purpose

- Describes the process for reviewing construction contract submittals submitted by general contractors or owner's representatives
- Part of the Corporate Quality Assurance Program
- Requirements to be implemented for all projects unless superseded by client/contract/project-specific requirements

## 2.0 Scope

- Applies to traditional review (when contract documents prepared by Hanson as POR)
- For nontraditional review (when contract documents prepared by other POR), refer to Attachment A
- Does not apply to professional design services delegated to contractors (covered by QUAL3094)

## 3.0 Definitions

Key terms include:

- **Actionable submittal**: Requires technical review and response by Hanson
- **Contract documents**: Contractor agreement, specifications, drawings, etc.
- **Informational submittal**: Does not require technical review, typically needs acknowledgment
- **Professional of Record (POR)**: Licensed professional with responsible charge
- **Record set**: Submittal prints/documents used for review and documentation
- **Submittal**: Shop drawings, product data, samples prepared by contractor/subcontractor

## 5.0 Responsibilities

### 5.1 Hanson Professional of Record

- Track and categorize submittals, maintain form [[QUAL3093.01]]
- Confirm contractor's review and approval prior to Hanson's review
- Confirm submittals meet content criteria
- Designate reviewers and establish review schedules
- Communicate scope of review to reviewers
- Affix review stamp and provide response

### 5.2 Reviewers

- Perform initial reviews of submittals
- Use review checklists if provided
- Transfer review notations from record set to copies
- Provide signatures on review stamp/forms as requested

## 6.0 Procedure

### 6.1 Receipt and Tracking of Submittals

- Accept submittals only from general contractor or owner's representative
- Date-stamp upon receipt
- Assign submittal numbers and maintain form [[QUAL3093.01]]
- Categorize as actionable or informational

### 6.2 Qualification of Submittal for Review

- Confirm submittal associated with Hanson-prepared contract documents
- Verify contractor has reviewed and approved submission
- Confirm submittal meets content criteria:
    - Doesn't request changes from contract documents
    - Significant changes have professional authentication
    - Not just copies of existing contract information
    - Free of extensive errors

### 6.3 Scope of Review and Schedule

- Review for conformance with Hanson's design intent and contract documents
- Initial review by designated reviewers
- Final review by POR or designee
- For informational submittals: acknowledge receipt or coordinate owner selection

### 6.4 Initial Review of Submittal

Suggested notation system:

- **Yellow highlighting**: No exceptions taken
- **Red circles/text**: Errors, omissions, corrections
- **Red text**: Notes of clarification
- **Green marks**: Items deferred to final review
- **Black "NOT REVIEWED"**: Items not designed by Hanson

### 6.5 Final Review of Submittal

- Performed by POR or designee
- Review items marked in green
- Document with initials and date on review stamp

### 6.6 Information Transfer

- Transfer review notations (except yellow and green) to copies
- Prepare specified number of copies

### 6.7 Review Stamp

- Apply review stamp to record set and copies
- Use Figure 1 format with six action categories:
    1. **No Exceptions Taken**
    2. **Furnish as Corrected**
    3. **Revise and Resubmit**
    4. **Submit Specified Item**
    5. **Rejected -- See Remarks**
    6. **For Information Only**

### 6.8 Record Set

- Becomes Hanson's project record
- Processed according to Section 7.0

### 6.9 Distribution

- Return appropriate number of copies to requesting party
- Include record of transmittal

## 7.0 Records

Processed per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]], consisting of:

- Form [[QUAL3093.01]] (or alternate form)
- Record set
- Copy of form [[QUAL3093.02]] (if applicable)
- Records of transmittal receipt and returns

## Attachment A: Procedure Modifications for Nontraditional Review

### Key Differences for Nontraditional Review:

- Contract documents prepared by POR other than Hanson (Owner's POR)
- Limited scope: check conformance with design intent and contract documents
- Use form [[QUAL3093.03]] exclusively (no review stamp)
- Alternative action language and terms
- Return to client or contractor as appropriate

This procedure establishes a comprehensive system for reviewing construction submittals while limiting Hanson's liability exposure and ensuring quality control throughout the construction phase.
