## 1.0 Purpose

- Provides requirements for generation, maintenance and final disposition of Hanson's project records
- Part of Corporate Quality Assurance Program (CQAP)
- To be implemented for all Hanson projects unless superseded by client/contract/project-specific requirements

## 2.0 Scope

- Applies to all project records except environmental samples and material samples assigned to Hanson SUBCONS
- Covers both digital and non-digital records
- Favors digital media due to ease and convenience
- Non-digital provisions useful for older projects and specific client requirements

## 3.0 Definitions

### 3.1 General

- **Retention period:** Time for which a record must be retained per [[CORP1300 - Records Management Manual]]
- **Record:** Information in hard copy or digital format created, received and maintained as evidence

### 3.2 Project Stage

- **Active stage:** During project performance from startup through completion
- **Inactive stage:** Project activities suspended/completed pending dormant stage
- **Dormant stage:** After project work activities and business processes completed

### 3.3 Digital Records

- **Administrative files:** Non-CAD files stored under "Admin" folder
- **CAD files:** Files stored under "CAD" folder
- **DMS:** Document management system (e.g., ProjectWise)
- **File:** Self-contained work created with application program
- **Project directory structure:** Notation identifying server/DMS location and folders

### 3.4 Non-Digital Records

- **Document:** Written/printed information including drawings, exhibits, photographs
- **Prototype:** Physical model or tangible rendering
- **Construction samples:** Physical samples of proposed constructed project
- **Environmental samples:** Physical samples of soil and water for chemical testing
- **Hard copy:** Record created and managed on paper or tangible media
- **Material samples:** Physical samples for classification and engineering tests

### 3.5 Non-Digital Record Storage

- **Active storage:** During active project stage under PM/TM oversight
- **Inactive storage:** During inactive/dormant stages under PM/TM oversight
- **Long-term storage:** Centralized locations during inactive/dormant stages

## 5.0 Responsibilities

### 5.1 Project Staff

- Generate/collect project records per scope of services and standard practice
- Maintain project records per procedure provisions

### 5.2 Information Technology (IT)

- Administer Hanson servers for active digital record storage
- Administer and retrieve digital records in inactive long-term storage
- Backup digital project records
- Protect against malicious alteration and virus
- Provide technical support
- Destroy digital records after retention period

### 5.3 Administrative Services

- Assist with active and inactive storage of non-digital records
- Coordinate transition from inactive to long-term storage

### 5.4 Corporate Management

- Provide inactive long-term storage for non-digital records
- Destroy non-digital records after retention period

## 6.0 Procedure

### 6.1 Generation of Records

- Records specified by scope of services, regulatory requirements, standard practice
- Must provide evidence of quality or have value for legal obligations/business transactions
- Records must be legible and traceable to project
- Licensed professional authentication required for external issuance

### 6.2 Receipt of Records

- Records from outside sources providing evidence of quality must be controlled
- Non-digital records must be date stamped upon receipt

### 6.3 Maintenance of Records

- Storage organized by project startup information and subject matter
- Avoid converting digital records to non-digital

#### 6.3.1 Digital Records

- Four-level organization system:
    1. Year project started
    2. Project/sub-project number
    3. Record type (Admin or CAD)
    4. Subject matter (Admin) or discipline (CAD)
- Protection through network administration controls
- Convert to PDF to maintain retrievability
- Read-only format for 5 years after dormant stage

#### 6.3.2 Non-Digital Records

- Five-level organization system:
    1. Project number (required)
    2. Sub-project (optional)
    3. Subject matter (required)
    4. Project file number (required)
    5. Project file name (optional)
- Protection from damage/loss in suitable environments

### 6.4 Records Submitted to or Generated by a DMS hosted by Others

- When using external DMS (e.g., client-hosted ProjectWise)
- **Critical requirement:** Retain copies of all information submitted to or received from external DMS within Hanson's own system
- Prevents irretrievable loss of important project records

### 6.5 Final Disposition of Records

- Per company policy after retention period
- Corporate management destroys non-digital records
- IT destroys digital records
