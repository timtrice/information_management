## 1.0 Purpose

- Provides details of Hanson's records management program
- Covers file retention, storage, and destruction
- Addresses federal and state legal requirements for record maintenance
- Warns of potential penalties, fines, and legal consequences for non-compliance

## 2.0 Scope

- Applies to paper and digital records created/received by Hanson management, staff, and board of directors
- Covers business transactions and legal obligations
- May be adopted for Hanson Group Inc. and affiliates/subsidiaries

## 3.0 Definitions

Key terms defined include:

- **Active storage**: Records for active projects/personnel requiring frequent access
- **Function**: Activities rather than operating units (preferred organization method)
- **Inactive storage**: Records for dormant projects/terminated employees with infrequent access needs
- **Office of record**: Individual/entity responsible for maintaining official record sets
- **Record**: Information in paper/digital format as evidence of business/legal activities
- **Record series**: Subcategories under corporate functions
- **Records curator**: Office of record for majority of physical inactive storage records

## 4.0 Record Organization

- **By Functions**: Accounting (ACC), Administration (ADM), Environment (ENV), Finance (FIN), Human Resources (HUM), Legal (LEG), Marketing (MAR), Projects (PRJ), Public Relations (PUB), Tax (TAX), Treasury (TRE)
- **Record Series**: Subcategories within each function with assigned retention periods
- **Office of Record**: Established for each record under given series
- **Table 1**: Summary of operating units, functions served, and offices of record

## 5.0 Record Storage

### 5.1 Active Storage

- **5.1.1 Physical/Paper Records**: Maintained at Springfield headquarters (except projects function at point of origin)
- **5.1.2 Electronic/Digital Records**: Stored on network drives, ProjectWise server, dedicated drives, or InfoServ with automatic Panzura backup

### 5.2 Inactive Storage

- **5.2.1 Physical/Paper Records**: Warehouse facility in Springfield; fireproof vault for limited records; annual shipment required for project records
- **5.2.2 Electronic/Digital Records**: Project files zipped/archived after 5 years of non-use; IT manages archived drives

## 6.0 Record Destruction

### 6.1 Physical Records

- Records curator reviews with stakeholders annually
- Destruction through appropriate methods (shredding service with certificates)
- Maintains list of destroyed projects
- Retains files with ongoing value or anticipated litigation

### 6.2 Electronic/Digital Records

- May retain digital project records beyond paper retention limits due to low storage cost
- Office of record maintains inactive digital records for company functions

## 7.0 Records

- Records curator maintains record of destroyed projects (not individual files)
