## 1.0 Purpose

- Describes the process for control of client/contract/project-specific quality assurance (QA) documents
- Ensures such documents and changes are reviewed for adequacy and approved for issue
- Part of the Corporate Quality Assurance Program (CQAP) approved by CQAO and president
- To be implemented for all client/contract/projects unless superseded by specific requirements

## 2.0 Scope

- **Applies to:** QA documents developed for use for a specific client or on a specific contract/project
- **Does not apply to:** Review of technical documents (covered by [[QUAL3092 - Preparation and Review of Technical Documents]])

## 3.0 Definitions

Key terms include:

- **Controlled document:** Subject to continuous updating with ongoing distribution records
- **Authentication:** Process of applying signature, date, and/or other notations
- **Document:** Written/printed information in paper or electronic format
- **Disposition:** Statement of project manager's proposed resolution of review comment
- **DMS:** Document management system
- **Draft document:** Ready for review but not for external release
- **Editorial review:** Review for format, presentation, spelling, and grammar
- **Final document:** Incorporates review comments and acceptable for release
- **Post/posting:** Filing document in project folder or adding to websites
- **Client/contract/project-specific QA document:** Specifies quality requirements for specific projects
- **Quality assurance review:** Review for conformance to applicable QA practices
- **Resolution:** Agreement between reviewer and project manager on comment disposition
- **Review comment:** Statement suggesting improvement or identifying problems
- **Uncontrolled document:** Issued for information only, not subject to updates

## 5.0 Responsibilities

### 5.1 Project Manager (or designee)

- Determining need for new documents
- Determining need for revision or discontinuance
- Providing guidance for development/revision
- Selecting originator, reviewers, and review coordinator
- Establishing review schedule
- Resolving/dispositioning reviewer comments
- Completing portions of form [[QUAL3110.01]]
- Posting final controlled documents
- Providing notification of new/revised documents

### 5.2 Originator

- Developing DRAFT documents per directions
- Preparing FINAL DRAFT documents based on accepted comments

### 5.3 Reviewer

- Reviewing DRAFT documents
- Providing review comments
- Resolving disagreements with project manager

### 5.4 Review Coordinator

- Distributing DRAFT documents to reviewers
- Monitoring review process and providing status updates
- Determining status of overdue reviews
- Assembling reviewer comments for project manager
- Completing portions of form [[QUAL3110.01]]
- Filing records

### 5.5 Quality Assurance Specialist

- Maintaining register of client/contract/project specific quality documents
- Coordinating with Hanson corporate business partners for register maintenance

## 6.0 Procedure

### 6.1 Process Steps (12 detailed steps)

1. **Document Development Decision:** PM determines need and appoints originator/reviewers
2. **Draft Preparation:** Originator prepares DRAFT document per PM/client direction
3. **Form Initiation:** PM completes sections I and II of [[QUAL3110.01]]
4. **Document Preparation:** Originator prepares DRAFT per guidance
5. **Review Coordination:** PM appoints review coordinator for distribution
6. **Review Execution:** Reviewers conduct timely reviews with comments
7. **Review Monitoring:** Coordinator tracks progress and contacts overdue reviewers
8. **Comment Resolution:** PM reviews comments and determines resolution/disposition
9. **Final Editing:** Document edited for grammar, spelling, and format
10. **Final Approval:** PM completes sections III-V of form, obtains officer signature if required
11. **Document Posting:** Final document posted per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]], section VI completed
12. **Control Documentation:** Controlled copies require authentication with documentation

### 6.2 Coordination With Reference Standards

Guidelines for documents that rely on reference standards:

- Identify each requirement/standard incorporated
- Identify instances where project-specific document supersedes reference standards
- Include definitions of terms requiring resolution
- Include language for resolving conflicts between documents and reference standards

## 7.0 Records

### 7.1 Project Records (per QUAL3130)

- Form [[QUAL3110.01]] (all revision levels)
- Final document (all revision levels)
- Documentation of staff notification
- Documentation of controlled copy issuance

### 7.2 Quality Assurance Records

- Register of client/contract/project specific quality documents
- Coordination records with corporate business partners
- Information collected during project startup process

## Associated Forms

- **[[QUAL3110.01]]:** Review, Approval and Posting Record
    - Includes general notes and instructions for completing form fields
    - Contains sections for document information, reviewers, superseded documents, new documents to be posted, approvals, and verification

This procedure ensures systematic control of project-specific quality documents while maintaining consistency with the corporate quality assurance program.
