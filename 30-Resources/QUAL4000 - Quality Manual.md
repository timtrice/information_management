### 1. Purpose

- Presents requirements of Corporate Quality Assurance Program (CQAP)
- Documentation for relevant parties (customers, certifying organizations)

## Key Features of the Quality Manual:

1. **Hierarchical Structure**: Three-tier document system (Quality Manual, Quality Procedures, Standard Practices)
2. **Comprehensive Coverage**: Addresses all aspects of quality management from planning through verification
3. **Integration**: Links quality assurance with project management and production processes
4. **Compliance Framework**: Provides structure for both internal and client-specific quality requirements
5. **Continuous Improvement**: Built-in mechanisms for auditing, assessment, and corrective action

This Quality Manual serves as the foundation document for Hanson's Corporate Quality Assurance Program, establishing the framework that is then detailed in the supporting Quality Procedures (QUAL3000 series documents).
