### 1.0 Purpose

- Provides requirements for preparation, review and approval of technical documents
- Part of Corporate Quality Assurance Program approved by CQAO and President
- To be implemented for all projects unless superseded by project-specific requirements

### 2.0 Scope

- Applies to technical documents issued to clients or other outside parties
- Can also be applied to non-technical documents at project manager's option
- Does NOT apply to:
    - Working papers, rough drafts, preliminary drafts
    - Documents issued "for information only"
    - Quality assurance documents

### 3.0 Definitions

Key terms include:

- **Disposition**: Statement of how a review comment is to be addressed
- **Draft document**: Document ready for review but not ready for release
- **Editorial review**: Review for format, presentation, spelling, grammar
- **Final document**: Document incorporating review comments, acceptable for release
- **Professional of Record (POR)**: Licensed professional with responsible charge
- **Technical document**: Document containing technical subject matter for specialized professional services
- **Technical review**: Documented review by qualified personnel independent of originators

### 5.0 Responsibilities

#### 5.1 Professional of Record/Task Manager/Sr. Team Member

- Designate personnel to originate and review documents
- Define scope of review including client requirements and interdisciplinary reviews
- Facilitate resolution between originator and reviewers
- Provide management review
- Provide professional authentication as required

#### 5.2 Originator

- Prepare draft document for review
- Initiate form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents|QUAL3092.01]]
- Address reviewer comments
- Revise draft document according to comment resolutions
- Accept editorial changes

#### 5.3 Reviewers

- Review draft document per designated scope
- Document review comments
- Reach agreement with originator on comment disposition

#### 5.4 Project Principal

- Facilitate resolution if POR is originator or reviewer

#### 5.5 Editor

- Edit documents for format, presentation, spelling, grammar

### 6.0 Procedure

#### 6.1 Review

- Originator consults with POR/task manager to determine if review is appropriate
- Procedure implemented if document meets definition of technical document OR project manager elects to use procedure

#### 6.2 Assigning Review

- Originator completes project/document information on form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents|QUAL3092.01]]
- Identifies document type and review scope
- Selects qualified reviewers
- Provides form and draft document to reviewers

#### 6.3 Performing Review

- Review can be hard copy with manual comments or electronic with digital comments
- Multiple reviewers follow sequential process
- Each reviewer signs and dates form upon completion
- Final reviewer submits document with all comments to originator

#### 6.4 Dispositions and Resolutions of Comments

- Originator reviews proposed revisions and comments
- If agrees: documents dispositions, makes revisions, returns to reviewer for approval
- If disagrees: documents disagreement, returns to reviewer
- If no resolution: POR resolves disagreement (or project principal if POR is involved)
- New content generated from comments must undergo same review process

#### 6.5 Editorial Review

- Draft document revised per comment resolutions
- Sent to editor for format, presentation, spelling, grammar corrections
- Editor signs and dates form upon completion
- Editorial revisions subject to originator approval

#### 6.6 Final Review

- POR completes management review for contract conformance and procedure compliance
- Documents requiring professional authentication are authenticated after management review
- Document becomes final and acceptable for issuance

### 7.0 Records

Records processed per [[QUAL3130 - Control of Project Records]] and consist of:

- Form [[QUAL3090.01 - Review of Technical Calculations, Technical Documents|QUAL3092.01]]
- Final document
