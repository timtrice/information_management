> [!question]
> How does this below differentiate between Project Classes?

DC shall validate the data per Project specifications and, if necessary, communicate with POR any conflicts. If all metadata meets standards, the document shall be uploaded into EDMS and considered the latest release. DC shall update metadata in Library, as necessary and, on completion, publish the final version. DC shall add a note of the transmittal number to the Publish comments.

If document does not meet quality standards document is to be considered "rejected" and returned to RE for correction.

When a deliverable is received from Client, DC shall perform a quality review analysis. On approval, DC shall place the deliverable into the DMS, updating metadata as required, publish the document with the incoming transmittal number, and with the DDM, email links to the new/revised documentation to Company. On disapproval, DC shall reject the transmittal and return to Client.
