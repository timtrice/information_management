## Definitions, Acronyms and Abbreviations

- **Access Log** - A log displaying all project personnel who have access to EDMS, their access privileges, the company to which they are employed, and an email address.
- **Client** - company with which project contract has been signed.
- **Company**: Hanson Professional Services, Inc.
- **Controlled Document**: A document that goes through internal review cycles but not submitted to Client.
- **CQAP**: Corporate Quality Assurance Program
- **Deliverable**: A document subject to continuous updating for which an ongoing record of its distribution and receipt (at all revision levels) is maintained on Client's behalf.
- **Document Control (DC)**: Responsible for performing quality assurance and checks on all controlled documentation.
- **Document Distribution Matrix (DDM)** - A lookup table showing a relationship between project personnel and the document types to which they will receive notifications.
- **Electronic Document Management System (EDMS)**: Data and documentation repository such as SharePoint. Network drives shall not be considered an efficient nor satisfactory EDMS.
- **IM/DC**: Information Management/Document Control services. Falls under the INF discipline.
- **Issue Status Code (ISC)**: Industry-standard codes identifying the status of document review cycles.
- **Main Information Contractor (MIC)**: A centralized service on Client's behalf to facilitate all angles of document management. Client and all subcontractors shall use Market EDMS or other review tools through document lifecycle.
- **Market** - The Industry market within Company.
- **Master Document Register** - A complete listing of all controlled documentation. For Class II and Class III projects, MDR shall maintain separate registries for Client and Market documentation.
- **O**: Originator - person who creates the document.
- **POR**: Professional of Record - professional licensed to practice in the jurisdiction who provides final approval.
- **QCR**: Quality Control Report - audit trail documentation from review sessions.
- **TM**: Task Manager - person responsible for managing review sessions prior to POR approval.
- **Uncontrolled Document**: Supporting documents that exist without formal QA/QC review processes.
