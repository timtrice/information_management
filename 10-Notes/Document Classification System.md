## Three Document Types

1. **Uncontrolled Documents**: Supporting documents without formal QA/QC review
2. **Controlled Documents**: Undergo internal review cycles but not submitted to Client (may turn into Deliverables)
3. **Deliverables**: Thorough review process, submitted to Client on a scheduled basis

Every controlled document shall have, at a minimum, a Company Document Number assigned. Where Client procedures and specifications mandate, a Client Document Number shall be used.

Where metadata terms may conflict, the metadata shall be differentiated between Company and Client, respectively. For example, Company Document Number and Client Document Number would be two different metadata tags.

Deliverables are not required to have a Company Document Number or other Company metadata assigned. Unless otherwise specified, only Client metadata shall be printed on the deliverable.
