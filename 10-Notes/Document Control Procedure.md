## Summary

The purpose of this document is to provide guidance on the concepts and processes of document control that are important for, and have impact on, the performance of Market projects and Company's business operations within Market.

This procedure defines the requirements for the creation, review, approval, distribution, use and revision of documentation for all Market projects where Information Management/Document Control (IM/DC) services may or may not be directly involved.

This procedure applies to all Market projects and serves as the minimum standard that all Market projects shall follow. Procedures are designed for ultimate simplicity while building good document management habits that prepare project teams for more complex requirements.
