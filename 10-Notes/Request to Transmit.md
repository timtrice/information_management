When a document revision draft has been completed, Originator shall review the document one final time to ensure the following:

- Metadata is present on the cover page of a document or in the title block of a drawing
	- Client document number
	- Document Title
		- Titles shall not contain symbols beyond hyphens, periods, parentheses, brackets, colons, and semi-colons.
		- Titles shall be title-case with the exception of acronyms which may be upper-case.
	- Client Document Revision (Reference Appendix 3)
		- Documents Issued for Review shall begin with revision A.
		- Documents Issued for Information shall begin with revision 0.
		- Approved documents that have undergone a review cycle shall begin with revision 0.
	- Issue Status Code (Reference Appendix 4)
	- Issue Date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	- Security Class (Reference Appendix 5)
- Sheet numbers shall not be included. Each drawing shall be submitted as its own drawing, sharing the same title with the exception the title is appended with " - Sheet *x* of *y*".
- For drawings, document metadata shall be in the lower-right corner of the drawing.
	- For documents, a cover sheet should identify the document title, document number, and revision. A revision block may be on the bottom, or on page 2.
- Ensure your documents are in natural reading order, i.e., top-left to bottom-right. Drawings and spreadsheets, as applicable, should be in landscape mode.
	- Documents must be fully legible and text-searchable.
- No document shall have a file embedded within it. Any atttachments shall be submitted with the original documents file name along with the text "-Att-*n*" where *n* is the attachment number. Attachments shall also be identified at the end of the parent document.
- If a document is to be issued VOID (Issue Status: SUP),
	- The document must have a clear watermark on the first page stating "VOID". Beneath the watermark shall be a reference to the superseding document, if any.
	- The superseding document shall be issued IFR, Revision A as if it were a new document. A stamp or other identifier shall be present on the first page noting the document supersedes the VOID document.
- Documents do not contain hyperlinks.

RE shall email native documents and PDFs to [documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com](mailto:documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com). PDFs must be OCRd and searchable.
