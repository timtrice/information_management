When a document has been issued that does not require a comment review cycle, or the comment review cycle has been closed, appropriate project personnel shall be notified of the document's existence within SharePoint. Every document and revision shall be associated with a transmittal. Transmittal shall clearly identify the transmittal number in the subject line, along with the status of the associated documents.

The body of the email shall contain a table of each documents file name, hyperlinked to the document within the DMS, along with

- Document Title
- Document Revision (Client takes precedence over Company)
- Revision Date
- Issue Status

The body of the email shall conclude with the following formatted text:

<span style="color: red; font-weight: 800;">THE MOST CURRENT VERSION OF PROJECT DOCUMENTS ARE ON THE PROJECTS RESPECTIVE SHAREPOINT SERVER. USERS OF THESE DOCUMENTS SHOULD ONLY TRUST THE PROJECTS RESPECTIVE SHAREPOINT AS THE OFFICIAL SOURCE. DIGITAL AND PRINTED COPIES OF THIS DOCUMENT STORED FOR ACCESS AND USE ANYWHERE ELSE MAY BE OBSOLETE.</span>

No other information is required.

The email shall be sent to all members as listed in <mark>[Document Distribution Matrix]</mark>.

The email shall be sent from the Hanson Document Control email address ([documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com](mailto:documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com)). No emails from individual email addresses shall be sent.

Once the email has been sent it shall be filed away in a subfolder of the inbox.
