Every Controlled document shall have a Company Document Number. This document number shall be provided by Document Control unless otherwise noted in Client procedures or specifications. If a new document is being produced and that document is not listed in the MDR, Project personnel shall submit a request for a new document number via email to [documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com](mailto:documentcontrol@hanson-inc.com?subject=Document%20Number%20Request%20Form). Document numbers shall only be assigned by Market Document Control.
