All documents shall have clearly identified Company Document Number, Company Document Revision, Company Revision Date, Revision History (up to three revisions), Company Issue Status, and Security Classification Code. Equipment tag numbers, if available, shall also be clearly identified.

For Class II projects, Client metadata shall be used in place of Company metadata, i.e., Client Document Number, Client Document Revision, etc.

<mark>Templates have been created to enable authors to immediately begin drafting a document. They may be found in the Project's shared i-drive or the Documents library in SharePoint.</mark>
