A Master Document Register (MDR) shall be created and maintained by Project DC. The MDR shall list all Deliverables planned and submitted to Client. Best practice is the MDR should be a full export via Microsoft Excel that is readily available for internal project personnel (see [[Document Control Procedure#Data Inventory|Data Inventory]]).

MDR shall list all Deliverables, regardless of confidentiality, along with all metadata identified in the MDR. DC shall perform audits on a weekly basis to ensure Deliverables are listed in both Library and the MDR, and that metadata is identical.

## Updating the MDR

Library shall be considered the most up-to-date register for all deliverables across the project. The MDR shall be considered a snapshot of EDMS.
