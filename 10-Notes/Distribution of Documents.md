Per the [[Document Distribution Matrix]], once a document has been released for transmittal, a review session will begin using Adobe Cloud. All users will receive a link to the document to review, mark up, and make any additional comments to the document.

Project personnel will have three days to review the document. At the end of the third day, the session will be closed and the document shall be uploaded to SharePoint with the Issue Status Code COM for "Comments". At that time, a new transmittal shall be created notifying all responsible Project personnel of the update with a link to the new documents in SharePoint.

## Adobe Cloud Review

> [!danger]
> Do to security concerns, no documents shall be transmitted or shared with anyone at any time. The following shall be considered outdated and no longer applicable.

Reviewers of documentation shall have an Adobe account. Users may visit [Adobe.com](https://www.adobe.com/) and click the "Sign in" button in the top-right corner of the home page. There is no fee for having an account with Adobe nor is any fee required on the Users end to comment and review documentation. However, Users with a paid Adobe Acrobat subscription may be able to review and comment on documents from their desktop app.

Users are encouraged to bookmark the homepage of the [Adobe Acrobate website](https://acrobat.adobe.com/link/home/). From there, Users can click "Documents" in the top navigation bar. On the left hand menu of the next page, under **FILES** is "Shared by others". All documents currently in the review cycle and requiring comments/review by User shall be listed here. Note once the comment review cycle closes the document will be removed from this page.

## Bluebeam Sessions

### Bluebeam Session Structure

1. **Originator (O)** creates the document
2. **Originator (O)** initiates Bluebeam review session per Document Distribution Matrix (DDM)
3. **Task Manager (TM)** and other reviewers participate in simultaneous review session
4. **Task Manager (TM)** finalizes review comments
5. **Professional of Record (POR)** provides final approval
6. **Originator** saves final marked-up document along with audit report to network drive.

### Standard Timeline for Review Sessions

- **Total duration**: 5 business days
- **Reviewer participation**: Days 1-4
- **TM finalization**: 24 hours prior to session closeout (Day 4)
- **POR final approval**: Final day (Day 5)

**Note**: POR may participate throughout the review cycle but has final approval authority.

### Session Management

1. **O** invites all project team members per DDM
2. All reviewers participate simultaneously in one session
3. Session prompts TM and other reviewers to begin review cycle
4. **Subcontractor participation** (when applicable): Comment-only access
	1. Requires Bluebeam subscription
5. Session closeout occurs at predetermined time

### Audit Trail Requirements

All projects must maintain audit trail of every controlled document:

- Marked-up PDFs with comments saved as controlled documents
	- These shall be identified with a code stamp per [[Document Control Procedure#Appendix D]]
- Bluebeam audit trail exports to Excel saved as separate QCR documents
- QCR documents receive independent sequential numbering

#### Audit Trail Management

##### Universal Requirement

All Market projects shall maintain an audit trail of every controlled document.

##### Audit Trail Components

**Two components shall be saved as controlled documents per review cycle:**

1. **Marked-up PDFs**: Complete document with all accepted review comments visible
2. **Bluebeam Audit Trail Export**: Excel export of review session activity.

##### Quality Control Reports (QCR)

Bluebeam audit trail exports shall be saved as separate QCR documents:

- QCR documents receive independent sequential numbering
- Example: `PRJ-PMG-QCR-241216` (audit trail for review session)
- QCR documents are linked to original documents through metadata

##### Documentation Requirements

1. **Session Completion**: When Bluebeam session ends, export audit trail to Excel
2. **Document Approval**: Save marked-up PDF as approved by POR
3. **Archive Storage**: Both components stored per network drive structure
4. **Metadata Tracking**: Critical for linking audit trails to original documents
