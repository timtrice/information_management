A generic Microsoft Visio template is also. Authors shall download the template to their working directory. From there, the following edits shall be made to the document.

1. Rename the file to the respective document number.
2. On the cover page, update
	a.     Document title in upper-right corner, and document title in center of the page
	b.     Document Number in upper-right corner
	c.     Security Class in upper-right corner
	d.     Export Code in upper-right corner
	e.     Revision block. The revision block shall be contained to no more than three rows with the most recent revision listed first.
		i.    Revision
		ii.    Issue Status
		iii.    Originator
		iv.    Reviewer
		v.    Approver
		vi.    (Revision) Date

Visio does not have headers and footers as Word and Excel do. Instead, the template uses a background drawing. In the template provided, the background is found on the tab "Border". This border must be edited as well for subsequent pages beyond the cover page. Only the top portion of the border shall be edited to match the data on the cover page.
