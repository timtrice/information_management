Project personnel - based on the DDM - shall be notified that a new document is available for review or comment. The review process shall be handled via Adobe Cloud. Reviewers will have three days to review a document. At the end of the third day, the review cycle shall be closed.

## For Information, For Use, Etc

Documents not requiring a review cycle shall be sent via email to appropriate project personnel. The email shall include all documents as listed in the transmittal, hyperlinked, along with required metadata ([Email Notifications](#_Email_Notifications)).
