## **Roles**

- **PM** – Project Manager
- **PP** – Project Principal
- **POR** – Professional of Record
- **OR** – Originator
- **RV** – Reviewer
- **RC** – Review Coordinator
- **ED** – Editor
- **QA-S** – QA Specialist
- **CQAO** – Corporate QA Officer
- **IT** – Information Technology
- **RCust** – Records Custodian

## Legend

- **R** – Responsible (does the work)
- **A** – Accountable (ultimate decision/approval)
- **C** – Consulted (provides input)
- **I** – Informed (kept updated)
